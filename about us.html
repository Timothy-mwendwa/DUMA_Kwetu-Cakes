About Us

Our Story

At Kwetu Cakes Company we supply Freshly Baked Cakes and Cupcakes for all occasions. Kwetu Cakes Company was established in 2012 when owner Jackline Valeria decided she wanted to turn her passion of cake decorating into a business from home where she could be close to her 6 home educated children. Jackline left school originally to become a florist. She now combines cake decorating & floristry to create beautiful masterpieces that help families in Geelong & surrounding suburbs to celebrate birthdays, weddings, engagements, christening, babies showers, gender reveals & anything else that needs a cake to be enjoyed.
